# Code of Conduct

CineWindows is committed to a respectful, focused, and harassment-free project community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful when discussing code, design, documentation, and user reports.
- Give actionable technical feedback and assume good intent while evidence is incomplete.
- Respect privacy, licensing restrictions, attribution, and responsible disclosure.
- Keep discussions relevant to CineWindows and accept maintainer decisions once they are final.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, publication of private information,
sustained disruption, and knowingly abusive or deceptive reports are not permitted.

## Scope

This policy applies to CineWindows repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions,
release channels, and project-related private communication.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [panditritesh2001@gmail.com](mailto:panditritesh2001@gmail.com). Include links,
dates, and enough context for a fair review. Do not publish private or sensitive evidence in an
issue. The maintainer may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or
restrict participation when needed to protect the project community.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Retaliation against a person
who makes a good-faith report is not permitted.

## Policy date

Effective and last reviewed: September 10, 2026.
